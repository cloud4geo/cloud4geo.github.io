Geospatial Infrastructure on Kubernetes

Deploy and scale your geospatial infrastructure with Kubernetes and Docker. Maximum availability, automatic scaling, and simplified management.

Modern and scalable infrastructure

Kubernetes allows you to orchestrate your entire geospatial infrastructure efficiently. From PostGIS databases to map servers, everything is deployed and scaled automatically according to demand.

What we do

  • Kubernetes architecture design for your geospatial stack
  • Containerization of services: PostGIS, GeoServer, MapProxy, etc.
  • Auto-scaling configuration based on load
  • High availability implementation with multiple replicas
  • Persistent storage configuration for geospatial data
  • Centralized monitoring and logging
  • CI/CD pipelines for automated deployments

Benefits

  • Automatic scaling: Adjust resources according to demand
  • High availability: Zero downtime
  • Simplified management: One system for all infrastructure
  • Optimized costs: Pay only for resources you use
  • Zero-downtime deployments: Updates without interruption
  • Portability: Works on any cloud or on-premise
